# Writing quality

- Front-load the point and use concrete language.
- Match the user's voice and avoid hype, filler, and em-dash overuse.
- Prefer plain English over corporate phrasing such as "leverage", "robust", or "seamless".
- Remove phrases that sound generated, including "in today's fast-paced world" and "it's not just X, it's Y".
